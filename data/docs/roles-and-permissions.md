# Roles and permissions

Every person in a Fernhill workspace has a workspace role, and every page has its own access settings. Together they decide what someone can see and change.

## Workspace roles

- Owner: everything an admin can do, plus billing. Only owners can change the plan, update payment details, download invoices and delete the workspace. A workspace always has at least one owner.
- Admin: manages members, groups, security settings, integrations and service tokens. Admins cannot see billing.
- Member: creates pages, tasks and spaces, and can invite guests if the workspace allows it.
- Guest: sees only the pages that are explicitly shared with them. Guests cannot create spaces, browse the member directory or open any other page in the workspace.

Enterprise workspaces can also create custom roles by combining individual permissions, such as a role that can manage integrations but not members.

## Guest limits

The number of guests depends on the plan:

- Free workspaces can invite up to 10 guests.
- Team workspaces can invite up to 100 guests.
- Business workspaces can invite up to 500 guests.
- Enterprise workspaces can invite unlimited guests.

## Page access levels

When you share a page, you choose one of four access levels:

- Full access: edit the page and change who can access it.
- Can edit: change the content, but not the sharing settings.
- Can comment: read the page and add comments.
- Can view: read only.

Pages inherit access from their parent page or space unless you change it.

## Spaces

Spaces group related pages and tasks. An open space can be joined by any member. A closed space is visible in the sidebar, but members must ask to join it. A private space is hidden from everyone who is not a member of it.

## Changing roles

Owners and admins change roles in Settings > Members. Changes take effect immediately, and open sessions pick up the new permissions within a minute. Only an owner can promote someone to owner.
