# Billing and invoices

Fernhill bills paid workspaces monthly or annually, depending on the billing cycle you chose. Annual plans are charged upfront for the full year. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time, and the change starts on your next billing date.

## What counts as a seat

You are billed for every active member in the workspace. Guests and suspended members are not billed and do not count as seats.

When you add members in the middle of a billing period, Fernhill charges a prorated amount for the remaining days on your next invoice. When you remove members, the unused time is added to the workspace as account credit, which is applied automatically to future invoices.

## Invoices

An invoice is created for every charge and emailed to the billing contact. To change the billing contact, update the billing email in Settings > Billing > Billing details.

Past invoices are available as PDF files in Settings > Billing > Invoices. Each invoice has a number in the format `INV-2026-004417`, lists every seat change in the period, and shows your tax ID if you added one.

## Payment methods and currencies

- Cards: Fernhill accepts Visa, Mastercard and American Express.
- Bank transfer: available for annual contracts of $10,000 or more, with net 30 payment terms.
- Currencies: invoices can be issued in USD, EUR or GBP. The currency is fixed for a workspace once its first invoice is issued.

## Taxes

Add your VAT or GST number in Settings > Billing > Tax details. Businesses in the EU with a valid VAT number are invoiced under the reverse charge rule, so no VAT is added. Sales tax for US customers is calculated from the billing address.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Fernhill emails the billing contact and retries the charge 3, 5 and 7 days after the first failed attempt. You can update the card at any time from Settings > Billing > Payment method, and the outstanding invoice is charged right away.

If every retry fails, the workspace becomes read-only 14 days after the first failed charge. Members can still view and export content, but nobody can create or edit pages until the balance is paid. Paying the invoice restores full access within a few minutes.
