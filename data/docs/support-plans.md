# Support plans

The support you get depends on your Fernhill plan. Every plan has access to the help center and the community forum at community.fernhill.app.

## Support by plan

- Free: help center and community forum. Questions are answered by the community, and by Fernhill staff when they can, with no response target.
- Team: email support, with first responses within 1 business day, Monday to Friday.
- Business: priority support by email and in-app chat, around the clock on weekdays. Severity 1 issues get a first response within 4 hours, Severity 2 issues within 8 hours, and other issues within 1 business day.
- Enterprise: Premium Support, available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for Severity 1 and 2 issues. A named customer success manager, a phone line for escalations and quarterly business reviews are included.

## Enterprise response targets

- Severity 1 issues get a first response within 1 hour.
- Severity 2 issues get a first response within 4 hours.
- Severity 3 issues get a first response within 1 business day.
- Severity 4 issues get a first response within 2 business days.

## Severity levels

- Severity 1: Fernhill is unavailable, or data is being lost, for many people in your workspace.
- Severity 2: a major feature, such as sync or sign-in, is broken and there is no workaround.
- Severity 3: something is not working as expected, but a workaround exists.
- Severity 4: a question, a how-to request or a feature request.

You choose the severity when you open a request. Support may change it after looking at the impact.

## Opening a request

Use Help > Contact support in any app, or email support@fernhill.app. Include:

- Your workspace ID from Settings > Workspace.
- The `X-Request-Id` value for API problems.
- The sync log for desktop sync problems.
- Screenshots or a screen recording when the issue is visual.

Response times are for the first reply from a support engineer, not for a fix. Automatic confirmation emails do not count as a response.
