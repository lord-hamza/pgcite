# Plans and pricing

Fernhill has four plans. Every plan includes the web, desktop and mobile apps, unlimited pages and tasks, the public API and the Slack integration. Paid plans are priced per member, and you can switch plans at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Free

The Free plan is for small groups getting started.

- The Free plan allows up to 5 members in one workspace.
- Free workspaces get 5 GB of total storage.
- Files can be up to 20 MB each.
- Support comes from the help center and the community forum.

## Team

Team costs $10 per member per month billed annually, or $12 per member per month billed monthly.

- Team workspaces can have unlimited members.
- Storage is 20 GB per member, pooled across the workspace.
- Files can be up to 1 GB each.
- The GitHub integration is included.
- Support is by email.

## Business

Business costs $18 per member per month billed annually, or $22 per member per month billed monthly.

- Everything in Team is included.
- Storage is 50 GB per member.
- SAML single sign-on and enforced two-factor authentication.
- Audit logs for owners and admins.
- A financially backed uptime SLA.
- Priority support with in-app chat.

## Enterprise

Enterprise is sold as an annual contract with a minimum of 50 members. Pricing depends on seat count and add-ons, so contact sales@fernhill.app for a quote.

- Everything in Business is included.
- Storage is set per contract, and files can be up to 5 GB each.
- SCIM provisioning, custom roles and IP allowlisting.
- Data residency in the region of your choice.
- A HIPAA business associate agreement.
- Premium Support with a named customer success manager.

## Trials and discounts

New workspaces can try Business free for 14 days without a credit card. When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless an owner picks a paid plan. If the workspace has more than 5 members at that point, the extra members keep read-only access until you upgrade or remove them.

Registered nonprofits get 50% off Team and Business annual plans. Apply from Settings > Billing > Discounts with proof of status.

All prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added at checkout based on your billing address.
