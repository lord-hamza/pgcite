# Refund policy

This policy covers self-serve Team and Business subscriptions. Enterprise agreements follow the refund terms in your order form.

## Annual plans

If you cancel an annual plan within 30 days of the initial purchase or of a renewal, Fernhill refunds the full amount of that charge. After 30 days, annual charges are not refundable, but you can turn off auto-renewal so the plan ends at the close of the current term.

Renewal reminders are emailed to the billing contact 10 days before an annual plan renews, so there is time to change seats or turn off auto-renewal.

## Monthly plans

Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. When you cancel, the workspace keeps its paid features until the end of the current billing month and then moves to the Free plan.

## Downgrades and seat changes

- Moving from Business to Team in the middle of an annual term converts the unused difference into account credit.
- Removing members creates prorated account credit, not a refund to your card.
- Account credit never expires while the workspace is active, and it cannot be paid out as cash.

## Service credits

Credits issued under the uptime SLA are handled separately from refunds. See the Uptime SLA article for how to claim them.

## How to request a refund

Only a workspace owner can request a refund. Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan and choose Request a refund, or email support@fernhill.app from the owner's address with the workspace ID.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. Bank transfer refunds are sent to the account that made the payment.

## Exceptions

Fernhill does not refund workspaces that were closed for violating the Terms of Service. Charges older than 12 months cannot be refunded or credited.
